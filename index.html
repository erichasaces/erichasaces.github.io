The reach board

A ninth-round kicker. A third-round quarterback. An eighth-round defense. Three quarterbacks on one roster.

Brandon Jamail — Chasing The Kupp

Cook and Henry in the first two rounds is a 2018 strategy. Aubrey in the ninth is a 2009 strategy. If this team wins, we are all converting to kickers-first philosophy and we will never speak of it again.

Sarah Weir — The Tight Ends

You named the team The Tight Ends, then used the fourth on Colston Loveland. Lamar in the third is all-in on weekly ceiling. Do not come looking for sympathy in Week 6.

James Huse — CeeDeez Dropzzzz

Gibbs and Bowers is a beautiful start. Then you drafted running backs like they were going out of stock and quarterbacks like the waiver wire does not exist.

Justin Garrard — Hurts so good

CMC at eight is the pick the whole industry argues about. If he plays 16 games you look like a genius. If he does not, TreVeyon Henderson is your RB2.

Eric Honeycutt — The Smoke Show

A defense in the eighth and Mahomes in the seventh is the most Texas draft sequence possible. Taylor, London, Pickens, Jacobs — that part slaps.

Calvin Huse — Rockin A Chubb!!

Not a roast so much as a PSA. Chase, Nico, and Marvin Harrison Jr. should be illegal in a family league.
